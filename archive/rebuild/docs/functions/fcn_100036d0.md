- **Normal completion**: consumes optional fields based on a flags byte, then sets `*(esi+0x38)` to either `0` or `0xFFFFFFFD` depending on `*(esi+0x3c)`.
- **Format/magic mismatch path**: if early checks fail, it adjusts counters at `*(esi)` / `*(esi+4)`, possibly sets `*(esi+0x58)=1`, and sets `*(esi+0x38)` to a boolean indicating whether `*(esi+4)==0`.

- A fixed-length portion is validated (`len==8` and high bits of a flags byte must be clear).
- Several optional sections are skipped depending on flag bits (0x04, 0x08, 0x10, 0x02).
