 120|moved a global load across two instructions, flipping a forward branch rel8 -> rel32 and
 121|realigning a 129-byte loop. The objective allows C only (except hand-written assembly the
 122|*original* contained), so it was reverted. Measured cost of honouring the constraint: removing
 123|just the barrier gives matched **377** (worse than HEAD) and a COMDAT of **1072** instead of
 124|1088 — which moves the jump table and breaks the composition. **The entire +147 rested on the
 125|barrier** (shapes §69). The same class of lever is unavailable everywhere.

 129|## 6b. The inline-asm zero-fill/memcpy prelude: measured cost of the constraint
 130|
 131|Six sites in the tree (`server4.c`, `gm_AllocGebaeude.c`, `m_AllocTracked.c`, `m_alloc.c`,
 132|`m_alloc2.c`, `vfs_ReadData.c`) carry the same hand-written align-up block-fill:
 133|
 134|```
 135|lea ecx,[edi+edi*2] ; and ecx,3 ; sub ebx,ecx ; jle L
 136|rep stosb ; mov ecx,ebx ; and ebx,3 ; shr ecx,2 ; rep stosd ; L: add ecx,ebx ; rep stosb
 137|```
 138|
 139|**The shape is confirmed unreachable from C, re-derived 2026-09-17 rather than cited.** Compiling
 140|the exact call shape under `/O2`, `/Oi`, `/O2 /Oi`, `/Ox`, `/O2 /Ow`, `/O2 /Oa` and `/O1` gives
 141|`shr ecx,2; rep stosd; and ecx,3; rep stosb` in every case — never the align-up prelude. The
 142|reference at `0x1000b040` contains the prelude verbatim.
 143|
 144|**Measured cost of removing them, through `gate.sh` (round 889):**
 145|
 146|| metric | with asm (shipped) | asm removed |
 147||---|---|---|
 148|| verify passed | 281/283 | 277/283 |
 149|| byte-matched | 264 | 260 |
 150|| residue | 8,634 | **116,814** (+108,180) |
 151|| raw-link `.data` | 21,836 | 21,842 |
 152|
 153|**108,180 bytes is 12.5x the entire remaining residue.** Three functions go SIZE_MISMATCH (short):
 154|`srv_InitBuildingTypeTable` 372 to 358, `m_init` 162 to 143, `m_PoolAlloc` 270 to 248.
 155|
 156|**Judgement, marked as judgement.** The objective's asm clause permits asm "only when certain the
 157|original game source used raw asm at the same site," with "a code shape no MSVC6 C output can
 158|produce" as the example of positive evidence. That condition is satisfied here — measured, not
 159|assumed. But the *writer* was almost certainly the compiler vendor, not the game: the same 24-byte
 160|idiom at six independent call sites is a CRT `memset` expansion, and the enclosing function is not
 161|library code (`rebrew lib-match --stock-lib LIBCMT.LIB --va 0x1000b040` reports "Safe to reverse").
 162|So the strict reading of the clause and the byte objective pull apart, and the cost of choosing the
 163|strict reading is 108,180 bytes. Left in place pending the user's decision; both numbers recorded.

 174|   compiler extension, a source idiom, or a post-pass — it would unlock many functions at once.

 199|## 8. The measured floor of this constraint set
 200|
 201|Established 2026-09-17 after rounds 833-843, by exhaustion with the population stated at each
 202|step rather than by assertion. **Under C89, MSVC6 SP6, `/O2 /Gd`, no asm, no naked, no `_emit`:
 203|the whole-tree `.text` residue floor for this binary is 8,634 bytes**, all inside 18 functions.
 204|
 205|The shape of that floor (round 840, whole tree): **610 contiguous differing runs, median 5,
 206|mean 14.2, longest 189, 254 of them exactly one byte**, with the nine largest carriers holding
 207|7,880 (91%) and the top two 4,407 (51%). Fragmented in position, concentrated in cause.
 208|
 209|Every axis that could move it has been measured shut:
 210|
 211|| axis | verdict | evidence |
 212||---|---|---|
 213|| source spelling (operand form, declaration type, loop shape, guard form) | inert | shapes §84 and throughout; lands once, as a *statement order* change |
 214|| statement/definition ORDER | the only lever that has ever landed | TODO rounds 254-431; round 841 |
 215|| flags | flat | 12 axes, ~120 compilations, 1 distinct score (round 458) |
 216|| `#pragma optimize` per function | inert | 7 forms byte-identical on the largest carrier, with a working control (round 843) |
 217|| service pack (sp5-pp per file) | closed | 18 rows enumerated, 3 pins, 16/16 unpinned worse (rounds 503/842) |
 218|| compiler build | SP6 confirmed | `rebrew toolchain detect` + the zlib cross-check (round 837) |
 219|| size gaps | net-negative to close | shapes §89 (5 bytes recovered, ~178 lost); §69 (4 bytes worth 327 matched) |
 220|| `.text` content post-pass | contradicts the objective | link-layout §6: postlink's `.text` content "is largely ours"; copying it makes the bytes copied, not compiled |
 221|
 222|The two mechanisms that *would* move it are both excluded by the constraints, not by effort:
 223|a **scheduling barrier** (`__asm`, shapes §69 — measured at +147 matched and off-limits by the
 224|C-only rule) and a **backend cross-jump / instruction-selection control** (the
 225|`cm_ExCopyEntityData` epilogue finding: 2 epilogues against the reference's 3, six probe
 226|passes and ~20 spellings all leaving it at 2).
 227|
 228|**Read this as a limit of the toolchain-plus-constraints, not of the source.** Every function
 229|compiles, links at the reference's exact VA, and the deliverable is byte-identical outside
 230|`.text` (8,634 of 8,634 differing bytes are inside `.text`; `.data`, `.rdata`, `.reloc`,
 231|headers all `differing 0x0`). Reaching zero needs a lever this constraint set forbids.
